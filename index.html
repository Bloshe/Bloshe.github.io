Who We Are

Three Decades of Excellence

Shahin Shahd Khorasan Co.

Operating under the Beloshe and Sanabad brands, we are a premier Iranian food manufacturer headquartered in Mashhad, Khorasan — producing and exporting world-class dairy products and frozen foods since 1995.

Our product portfolio spans Pizza Cheese Toppings, Pasteurized Animal Butter, Ghee / Clarified Butter, IQF Vegetables & Fruits, IQF French Fries, and Aseptic Tomato Paste, exported to markets across the region.

Committed to uncompromising quality, every product carries the Beloshe seal of premium craftsmanship.
